A good Friday

Panel 1

Sunlight falls across a school classroom on a Friday afternoon.

I thought this was going to be the best Friday ever. Until now.

Panel 2

The narrator and Jeremy sit together at their classroom desks with their finished schoolwork.

My friend Jeremy and I had finished our schoolwork. We were both happy.

Panel 3

The two friends grin at their computers while colorful cars race across a typing-game screen.

We were racing on Nitro Type, enjoying our fun Friday.

Panel 4

The classroom loudspeaker interrupts the afternoon. The students look up in alarm.

Then the announcement came on.

Principal: Lockdown. This is not a drill.

Lockdown

Panel 5

Frightened students huddle in a corner, away from the classroom door.

Everyone started to panic. We ran to the corner of the classroom.

Panel 6

Jeremy and the narrator listen anxiously to the silence beyond the closed classroom door.

We wondered if we would live another day. We couldn’t tell whether anyone was outside.

Panel 7

One frightened classmate calls out while the others turn toward them in alarm.

Then the most annoying person in our class shouted. They didn’t know he was right outside.

Panel 8

The closed classroom door shakes violently in its frame.

Someone started ramming the door.

Sound: BANG! BANG!

The door

Panel 9

The classroom door breaks inward and a masked intruder stands in the opening as students flee. No weapon is visible.

The door came down. Everybody ran.

Sound: CRASH!

Panel 10

Jeremy freezes beside a desk while the other students hurry away.

But Jeremy was too shocked to move.

Panel 11

The narrator pushes Jeremy toward the escape route as the intruder approaches.

Instead of running, I decided to save him—even if it meant sacrificing myself. I pushed Jeremy out of the way.

Panel 12

Jeremy escapes into the corridor while the masked intruder seizes the narrator by the shoulder. No injury is shown.

Jeremy snapped out of it and ran. The intruder took me and left the others.

The van

Panel 13

The closed rear doors of an ominous black cargo van stand in the school’s shadow. What happened inside is left unseen.

He tied me up, taped my mouth, and put me in his dark, creepy black van.

Panel 14

A narrow strip of daylight crosses the narrator’s frightened eyes inside the moving van.

I was so scared I could hardly move.

Panel 15

From the dark interior, the newly opened van doors reveal the captor silhouetted against an unfamiliar yard.

When the van finally stopped, he took me out.

Panel 16

The captor reveals an unfamiliar, threatening face, holding his removed mask and jacket.

He took off his mask and jacket. I didn’t know him, but he didn’t look friendly.

A horror movie

Panel 17

The narrator’s frightened face is seen beside the black van while the captor’s long shadow falls across the ground.

I stood there, afraid of my own shadow.

Panel 18

The unfamiliar captor leans forward and threatens the narrator. His expression is cruel.

Captor: I’m going to make your life a horror movie.

Panel 19

The van doors close again as the captor walks away laughing.

He laughed and laughed and laughed. Then he left me tied up in the van.

Sound: HA. HA. HA.

Panel 20

Now free, the narrator begins opening the van’s rear door from the inside.

After about an hour, I managed to untie myself.

The key

Panel 21

The narrator steps out of the van and sees several threatening figures spread across the yard.

I ran out—only to find the van surrounded by more people.

Panel 22

The narrator races toward a parked car at the edge of the yard.

I ran and found a car.

Panel 23

The narrator searches the front of the car while a hand extends a key from behind the seat.

Inside, I searched for the key.

Stranger: You looking for the key?

Panel 24

The narrator takes the offered key, then turns in sudden fear toward a stranger seated behind them.

Then I looked behind me—and screamed.

Narrator: Thank you.

Run

Panel 25

A threatening stranger looms behind the front seat with a knife held at his side. The frightened narrator is in the foreground. No contact or injury is shown.

There was a man with a knife.

Panel 26

The narrator ducks toward the open car door as the attacker’s arm passes overhead. No contact is shown.

I ducked his first attack and tried to get out of the car.

Panel 27

Seen from outside the car, the narrator recoils and clutches an arm. The attack and wound remain outside the frame.

As I tried to escape, he threw the knife. It hit me in the arm.

Panel 28

The narrator runs away from the car while holding their injured arm against their body. Clothing covers the injury.

I screamed in pain. I couldn’t move my arm. But I kept running.

Home

Panel 29

The narrator rides a bicycle away along a quiet street, keeping the injured arm close to their body.

I found a bike and rode home.

Panel 30

The narrator stops outside their home. Broken windows and scattered red stains suggest something terrible has happened; no people, bodies or injuries are shown.

When I got there, the windows had holes in them. There were red splashes everywhere.

Panel 31

The narrator reaches Jeremy’s house and finds its windows broken too, with red marks on the exterior. Nobody is visible.

I ran to Jeremy’s house. It was the same.

Panel 32

The narrator’s stunned face is reflected in Jeremy’s cracked window as evening falls. The reflection leaves what happened inside unseen.

That man had really turned my life into a horror movie.
