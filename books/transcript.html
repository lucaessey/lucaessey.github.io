From Yips to Nerps

1. Life on Flummoxprap

Panel 1

A purple-and-yellow planet hangs in space, its hills dotted with gray rock homes.

On a purple and yellow planet called Flummoxprap lived creatures unknown to the rest of the galaxy.

Panel 2

Hooded Yips sing together, grinning beneath long noses. Their eyes disappear into the shadows of their hoods.

They were called Yips. They were musical. They were peaceful. They were very easy to quote.

Yip: Yip!

Yip: Yip!

Yip: Yip!

Panel 3

Bare toes shuffle rapidly beneath the hems of long robes.

They got around by shuffling their bare toes. Inch by inch. Surprisingly quickly.

Sound: shuffle shuffle

Panel 4

A smiling Yip opens a gray rock home on a purple hillside.

Home was under a rock. A perfectly good rock.

Panel 5

A yellow street curls up and down purple hills between clusters of gray boulders.

The streets went up, down, around—and then around some more.

Panel 6

Purple-and-yellow banners decorate the rock shops and cinema along a busy street.

The shops were rocks. The movie theaters were rocks. The entertainment facilities? Also rocks.

Labels: SHOPS, CINEMA

Panel 7

A cup of purple ploofidonk and a yellow brog patty sit on a café table.

They drank purple ploofidonk and ate yellow brog. Brog was shaped suspiciously like a patty.

Labels: PLOOFIDONK, BROG

Panel 8

Two Yips gesture enthusiastically to each other, both wearing enormous toothy smiles.

Conversation was simple. “Yip” was the only word they knew.

Yip: Yip?

Yip: Yip!

2. Some very polite visitors

Panel 9

A lime-green, blimp-shaped spaceship looms over the Yips’ town.

Then, one day, the Gorfs arrived.

Panel 10

A huge green Gorf holds a tiny orange pistol. Bubbles and distorted scenery are visible through its translucent goo.

Gorfs were great green blobs of gooey acid. Their little orange weapons looked a lot like water pistols.

Panel 11

The Gorf leader bends down to address a cheerful Yip.

The Gorf leader spoke in a low, scary British voice. His manners were impeccable.

Gorf: Could you take me to your leader?

Yip: Yip!

Panel 12

A Yip leads the Gorf to the largest rock in town, where the royal Yip waits.

The Yip shuffled toward the biggest Yip rock of all.

Gorf: Good day, sir? Ma’am? Um… Royal Yip.

Panel 13

The Gorf leader bows before the smiling royal Yip and the assembled court.

Gorf: Is there any way you would allow us to take over your planet?

Panel 14

The translucent Gorf speaks politely beside the still-cheerful royal Yip.

Gorf: I really don’t want to be a bother. I just need to destroy anything and everything on Flummoxprap.

Yip: Yip.

Panel 15

The Gorf holds its orange weapon toward a smiling Yip outside a rock home.

That was all the Yip leader knew how to say. So the Gorf leader went around town.

Gorf: May I destroy you?

Yip: Yip.

Panel 16

Another Yip smiles at the Gorf outside a rock shop while more Yips wait behind.

The answer was always the same. Until…

Gorf: May I destroy you?

Yip: Yip!

3. Five little disagreements

Panel 17

Five Yips in distinct purple-and-yellow robes stand together before the Gorf.

Gorf: May I destroy you?

Panel 18

The tall, pointed-hood Yip’s smile has become a short, downturned line. The other four stand behind.

Five Yips learned a new word.

Nerp: Nerp.

Panel 19

All five Nerps stand in a row with gloomy mouths and their eyes hidden in shadow.

Each of the five said it. And so they were forever known as the Nerps.

Nerp: Nerp.

Nerp: Nerp.

Nerp: Nerp.

Nerp: Nerp.

Nerp: Nerp.

Panel 20

A broad Yip grin is shown beside a Nerp’s small frown.

Much the same robes. Much the same noses. Considerably less cheer.

Panel 21

The five Nerps approach the open entrance of an enormous gray rock spaceship.

The five Nerps boarded their own ship. It was, of course, a comically large rock.

Panel 22

A large red button sits among many small yellow buttons on a purple control console.

Among all the buttons marked “Yip” was one large red button.

Labels: Yip, Yip, Yip, Away

Panel 23

A plum-robed sleeve presses the red button while the other four Nerps watch.

Its sticker read “Away.” One Nerp pressed it.

Sound: CLICK

Panel 24

The giant rock ship flies away from Flummoxprap into space.

And away they went.

4. A three-part plan

Panel 25

Five silent Nerps watch their purple-and-yellow home recede through the ship’s window.

Their journey was long and silent, punctuated only by their new, grumbled word.

Nerp: Nerp.

Panel 26

Earth fills the window as the Nerps gather around a planning table.

Earth drew closer, and so did the hope of a new home. The Nerps were gloomy, but smart: they suspected they would not be welcome. They devised a plan. Three things.

Panel 27

A robed sleeve opens a drawer containing an invisibility button.

One: the button hidden in a drawer.

Labels: Invisibility

Panel 28

A huge inflatable sumo costume is laid out, with places for a Nerp in each limb and one in the center.

Two: an inflatable sumo wrestler costume. One Nerp per limb, and one in the middle as the head.

Panel 29

A Nerp finds an empty weapon rack where a laser gun should have been.

Three: the mighty LASER GUN! …Which they had not packed.

Sound: …

Panel 30

The Nerps solemnly examine a brightly colored toy foam-dart gun.

They had to settle for a Nerf foam-dart gun. Since they could only say “Nerp,” that was what they called it.

Nerp: Nerp.

Panel 31

The five Nerps prepare the invisibility button, inflatable costume, and small toy gun.

One invisible rock. One lumpy disguise. One “Nerp.” Time to execute the plan.

Panel 32

The rock ship fades to a ghostly outline as it approaches Earth.

They pressed the button. No one but the Nerps could see their abnormally large rock.

5. An entirely ordinary arrival

Panel 33

A Japanese city street surrounds a 7-Eleven with a flat roof.

Their invisible rock came to rest on the roof of a 7-Eleven in Japan.

Labels: 7-ELEVEN

Panel 34

The five Nerps stand on the store’s roof beside the faint outline of their invisible ship.

Phase one: complete. Phase two was going to be a tight fit.

Panel 35

The Nerps unfold an enormous, empty sumo wrestler costume.

They unfolded the costume.

Panel 36

Four Nerps squeeze awkwardly into the costume’s arms and legs as the fifth waits.

One Nerp per limb…

Nerp: Nerp!

Nerp: Nerp!!

Panel 37

The final Nerp squeezes into the center of the now-lumpy costume.

…and one in the middle as the head. After plenty of painful “Nerps,” all five fit.

Nerp: Nerp!!!

Panel 38

The inflatable disguise waddles along the street with its limbs at awkward angles.

They waddled into the city. Their walking was not even remotely human.

Sound: wobble

Panel 39

Passersby give the unusually lumpy costume puzzled glances.

There were looks. Quite a few looks.

Panel 40

The costume waits at elevator doors, with the tiny toy gun poking out.

Phase three: shoot anyone who discovered their true identity. There was one small problem: their “Nerp” gun could only mildly annoy someone.

6. A crushing victory. Apparently.

Panel 41

Inside the elevator, the costume presses buttons to ride up and down.

They rode the elevator up and down. Just for fun.

Panel 42

An elderly man with glasses and a cane joins the costume in the elevator.

An elderly man joined them. Even with his glasses, he could barely see more than a large sumo wrestler.

Panel 43

A long-nosed Nerp’s hooded head suddenly pokes out of the costume.

Then a head popped out.

Nerp: Nerp!

Panel 44

A soft foam dart lightly hits the elderly man’s jacket.

A direct hit. A very soft direct hit.

Sound: piff.

Panel 45

Unharmed, the man adjusts his glasses and looks down in confusion.

The man was confused. The Nerps were proud. Then the Nerps were confused: he was still standing.

Panel 46

More soft darts bounce off the man’s coat and scatter on the elevator floor.

So they shot him again. And again. And again.

Sound: piff. piff. piff.

Panel 47

The doors open, and the man calmly leaves the elevator with his cane.

Eventually, the man got out.

Sound: DING

Panel 48

The Nerp peers from the costume, toy gun raised, surveying the now-empty doorway.

Maybe he was intimidated? Probably it was just his floor. The Nerps considered the operation a success.

Nerp: Nerp.

7. Home, gloomy home

Panel 49

The costume waddles out into a street darkening with evening shadows.

It was getting late. They still had nowhere to stay.

Panel 50

A small house sits beneath a bridge on a quiet side street.

Then they saw it. On a quiet street where few people went. Beneath a small bridge.

Panel 51

The house has faded navy-blue walls, a rusty white fence, a hazy window, and a patch of dead grass.

A faded navy-blue house. A white fence, rusty and chipped. A little patch of dead grass. Perfect.

Panel 52

A crooked warning sign hangs from the chipped white fence.

And an extremely promising sign.

Labels: DO NOT TRESPASS

Panel 53

The five Nerps, now out of the costume, straighten the warning sign.

They straightened it. A home should have standards.

Labels: DO NOT TRESPASS

Panel 54

The front window looks hazy from the street, with the sofa barely visible close to the glass.

From outside, you could only see things close to the window. From inside, you could see everything.

Panel 55

The five Nerps enter a small doorway and discover an impossibly enormous room.

Inside, the house was HUGE. At least five times bigger than it looked from the street.

Panel 56

A large sofa stands beside the front window. A dark doorway opens in the back-right corner.

One big sofa stood right by the window. In the back-right corner, a dark doorway led into an enormous back room.

8. A most unfortunate business plan

Panel 57

The five Nerps huddle conspiratorially in the huge back room.

The Nerps had the best idea. They huddled together for a minute.

Nerp: Nerp.

Nerp: Nerp?

Nerp: Nerp.

Panel 58

Covered sleeves work rapidly on a strange conveyor machine.

Then they got to work. About ten seconds later, their masterpiece was finished.

Sound: clank clank

Panel 59

An empty human-sized hopper feeds a long conveyor belt ending at a table.

A bin big enough for a whole human. A conveyor belt across the room. A table at the end.

Panel 60

A Nerp holds the same harmless toy gun in the dim room, with soft darts nearby.

Their plan was to shoot anyone who trespassed until that person could no longer stand—or breathe. The weapon was still the same harmless foam-dart gun.

Panel 61

The machine’s completely empty hopper leads into its dark interior.

Then, in their plan, the trespasser would go into the machine. And somehow…

Panel 62

A small open cardboard box holds one toy gun and exactly three foam darts.

…out would come a small brown cardboard box, containing one Nerp gun and three Nerp darts.

Panel 63

The five Nerps shuffle from the back room toward the sofa.

With their work complete, the five Nerps returned to the front room.

Panel 64

Seen through the front window at night, all five Nerps sit together on the sofa, waiting. Nobody approaches.

And so they sat on the sofa, which easily held all five Nerps, and waited for their first victim.
