From Yips to Nerps

All eight chapters, with narration, dialogue, signs, sound effects, and descriptions of the illustrations. Read the illustrated comic.

1. Life on Flummoxprap2. The politest invasion3. One new word4. A three-part plan5. Nothing unusual here6. An impressive victory7. The perfect house8. The waiting room

1. Life on Flummoxprap

Panel 1

Illustration: Purple-and-yellow Flummoxprap hangs in space, its hills dotted with little gray rocks.

Somewhere in the galaxy, on a purple-and-yellow planet called Flummoxprap, lived creatures unknown to the rest of the universe.

Panel 2

Illustration: Smiling robed Yips shuffle along winding yellow streets through purple hills and gray rock homes.

They were the Yips. Their streets wound up hills, down hills, and around hills that probably did not need going around.

Yip: Yip!

Another Yip: Yip!

Panel 3

Illustration: Gray boulder businesses line a busy purple-and-yellow street with decorative banners.

Homes were rocks. Shops were rocks. The movie theater? Also a rock. On the busy streets, decorative banners helped everyone tell the rocks apart.

Signs and labels: BROG & PLOOFIDONK · THE YIP PICTURE HOUSE

Panel 4

Illustration: Close view of a Yip with a drooping nose, a broad toothy grin, and complete darkness beneath the brow of the hood.

A Yip wore a long hooded robe. Only a long, drooping nose, a full toothy smile, and bare toes escaped it. The eyes stayed completely hidden in shadow.

Panel 5

Illustration: Bare toes shuffle rapidly under long robe hems.

Walking was a rapid, inch-by-inch shuffle of toes. It was surprisingly efficient. It was not especially dignified.

Sound effect: shuffle shuffle shuffle

Panel 6

Illustration: A smiling Yip raises a glass of purple ploofidonk beside a yellow brog patty.

Instead of water, they drank purple ploofidonk. They ate brog: a yellow substance shaped very much like a patty. Life had its essentials.

Panel 7

Illustration: Five Yips with distinct purple-and-yellow robe markings enjoy brog together.

Yips were musical creatures. They could greet, gossip, sing, and discuss lunch with one remarkably versatile word.

Yip: Yip?

Yip: Yip!

Yip: Yip, yip, yip!

Panel 8

Illustration: A peaceful musical town of gray rocks settles into a purple-and-yellow sunset.

And so their peaceful little world went on. Yip after yip after yip.

Distant Yips singing: Yip! Yip! Yip!

2. The politest invasion

Panel 9

Illustration: A lime-green blimp-shaped spaceship casts an enormous shadow over the rock town.

Until one day, a lime-green spaceship arrived. It looked an awful lot like a blimp.

Panel 10

Illustration: A large green Gorf, with bubbles and distorted scenery visible through its gelatinous body, holds a tiny orange pistol in a gooey protrusion.

Out came the Gorfs: great, green, translucent blobs of gooey acid. They carried small orange weapons that looked suspiciously like water pistols.

Panel 11

Illustration: The translucent Gorf leader leans courteously toward a tiny smiling Yip.

Their voices were low, scary, and very British. Their manners were impeccable.

Gorf leader: Could you take me to your leader?

Yip: Yip!

Panel 12

Illustration: A Yip leads the enormous green Gorf up a winding street toward the largest gray rock.

The Yip shuffled toward the biggest Yip rock of all.

Panel 13

Illustration: Beneath the royal rock, the Gorf bows to the Yip leader on a purple-and-yellow throne.

The Gorf leader made a very respectful introduction.

Gorf leader: Good day, sir? Ma’am? Um… Royal Yip. Is there any way you would allow us to take over your planet?

Panel 14

Illustration: The Gorf leader gestures politely with a tiny orange weapon while the Yip leader listens.

A small request. Practically a favor.

Gorf leader: I really don’t want to be a bother. I just need to destroy anything and everything on Flummoxprap.

Panel 15

Illustration: The Yip leader beams a completely oblivious toothy smile.

The Yip leader considered this with all the vocabulary at their disposal.

Yip leader: Yip.

Panel 16

Illustration: The Gorf courteously visits smiling Yips along a street of gray rock homes.

So the Gorf went around town, checking with everyone individually. It was important to be polite.

Gorf leader: May I destroy you?

First Yip: Yip!

Gorf leader: May I destroy you?

Second Yip: Yip!

Gorf leader: May I destroy you?

Third Yip: Yip!

3. One new word

Panel 17

Illustration: Five Yips with a stripe, violet hem, zigzag, shoulder patch, and three dots face the Gorf as their smiles disappear.

But five Yips had learned something new.

Gorf leader: May I destroy you?

Stripe: Nerp.

Hem: Nerp.

Zigzag: Nerp.

Patch: Nerp.

Dots: Nerp.

Panel 18

Illustration: The five gloomy Nerps stand shoulder to shoulder, refusing the towering translucent Gorf.

From then on, those five were known as the Nerps. There were exactly five of them. Their enthusiasm had also left in a group.

Panel 19

Illustration: Close-up of the striped Nerp’s tiny downturned mouth, long nose, and shadow-hidden eyes.

They looked much the same as Yips, except their mouths were now small, almost straight lines with a slight downward curve.

Nerp: Nerp.

Panel 20

Illustration: Exactly five marked Nerps hurry on bare toes toward an enormous gray rock.

They shuffled straight to their own spaceship.

Sound effect: shuffle shuffle shuffle

Panel 21

Illustration: Five tiny Nerps climb a ramp into an absurdly large plain gray boulder spaceship.

Their spaceship was, naturally, a comically oversized rock.

Panel 22

Illustration: Purple-and-yellow controls surround a huge red central button.

The controls were reassuringly simple. Every button said “Yip”… except the big red one in the middle.

Signs and labels: Yip · Yip · Away · Yip · Yip

Panel 23

Illustration: The striped Nerp presses the giant red button while the other four brace themselves.

One Nerp pressed the button with the sticker that read “Away.”

Nerp: Nerp!

Signs and labels: Away

Panel 24

Illustration: The enormous gray rock spaceship blasts away from purple-and-yellow Flummoxprap.

And away they went.

4. A three-part plan

Panel 25

Illustration: A lonely gray rock travels through sweeping galaxies, with its home planet far behind.

The journey was long and quiet. Flummoxprap’s colors faded into the distance. The silence was occasionally interrupted by a grumbled…

Inside the ship: Nerp.

Panel 26

Illustration: Five gloomy Nerps look through their cockpit window toward Earth.

As Earth grew closer, so did their hope of a new home. The Nerps were gloomy, but very smart. They suspected they would not be welcome.

Panel 27

Illustration: The five Nerps huddle over a plan with three parts.

They contrived a plan. A plan consisting of three things.

Nerp: Nerp?

Nerp: Nerp.

Nerp: Nerp!

Panel 28

Illustration: An open cockpit drawer reveals a special purple-and-yellow button.

ONE: the button hidden in a drawer. The one marked “Invisibility.” Convenient, that.

Signs and labels: Invisibility

Panel 29

Illustration: A cutaway shows five Nerps packed into a single inflatable costume: one per arm, one per leg, and one in the head and torso.

TWO: one inflatable sumo wrestler costume. Large enough for all five Nerps: one in each limb, and one in the middle as the head.

Panel 30

Illustration: A Nerp looks into an empty equipment case where the laser gun should have been.

THREE: a weapon well known on Flummoxprap—THE LASER GUN! Unfortunately, they had not had time to pack a laser gun.

Panel 31

Illustration: The Nerps present a blue-and-yellow toy gun with an orange tip and three blunt foam darts.

So they settled for a foam-dart gun. Since they could only say “Nerp,” that became its name. The Nerp gun.

Nerp: Nerp.

Panel 32

Illustration: A sleeve presses the drawer button as the rock spaceship becomes an invisible outline above Earth.

Phase one: press the hidden button. Their enormous rock became a sneaky vehicle that nobody but the Nerps could see.

Signs and labels: Invisibility

5. Nothing unusual here

Panel 33

Illustration: An invisible rock, indicated by a dotted outline for the reader, settles on a Japanese 7-Eleven roof.

They landed their invisible rock on the roof of a 7-Eleven in Japan. An entirely ordinary place to park a gigantic rock.

Signs and labels: 7-ELEVEN

Panel 34

Illustration: The five distinct Nerps stand on the roof beside one enormous empty inflatable costume.

Phase two: get five Nerps into one costume.

Panel 35

Illustration: Two Nerps struggle into the costume’s limbs while the other three wait.

This involved considerably more squishing than the plan had suggested.

Nerp in a sleeve: Nerp!

Nerp in a leg: Nerp!!

Waiting Nerp: Nerp?

Panel 36

Illustration: Five cramped Nerps fill the costume, one in each limb and one in the center.

Eventually, after several painful “Nerps,” all five fitted. More or less.

Five cramped Nerps: Nerp…

Panel 37

Illustration: The inflated lumpy costume awkwardly squeezes through a rooftop stairway.

They set off.

Sound effect: squeeeak

Panel 38

Illustration: People in an ordinary Japanese shopping street give puzzled looks to the clumsy inflatable disguise.

Their waddle was not remotely like a human walk. People stared. A great deal of the staring was deserved.

Sound effect: wobble · waddle · wobble

Panel 39

Illustration: A hidden sleeve grips the colorful toy gun behind the inflatable costume.

Phase three: shoot anyone who discovered their true identity. They did not know a foam-dart gun could do little more than mildly annoy someone.

Panel 40

Illustration: The lumpy costume approaches an elevator inside a city building.

Then they found an elevator. They decided to ride it up and down for fun. Even Nerps needed a hobby.

6. An impressive victory

Panel 41

Illustration: The inflatable costume stands in an elevator beside an elderly man with thick glasses.

An elderly man joined them. He could barely see, even with his glasses. As far as he could tell, he was sharing an elevator with a very large sumo wrestler.

Panel 42

Illustration: A Nerp’s sleeve reaches from the costume to press elevator buttons.

Up. Down. Up. Down. An excellent afternoon.

Inside the costume: Nerp!

Panel 43

Illustration: The striped Nerp’s long-nosed hooded head accidentally protrudes from the inflatable costume.

Until one head popped out.

Exposed Nerp: Nerp?!

Panel 44

Illustration: A soft orange foam dart gently bounces off the elderly man’s cardigan without hurting him.

Phase three! A direct hit!

Sound effect: piff

Panel 45

Illustration: The elderly man peers in bewilderment through thick glasses at a harmless dart on the floor.

The man wondered what had just hit him. The Nerps wondered why he was still standing.

Inside the costume: Nerp?

Panel 46

Illustration: More soft foam darts bounce harmlessly off his cardigan and gather around his shoes.

So they shot him again. And again. And again. He remained extremely un-defeated.

Inside the costume: Nerp! Nerp! Nerp!

Sound effect: piff. piff. piff.

Panel 47

Illustration: The elderly passenger calmly leaves at his floor as the inflatable costume watches.

Eventually, the doors opened and the man got out.

Sound effect: ding

Panel 48

Illustration: Alone in the elevator among scattered foam darts, the exposed Nerp looks perplexed and proud.

Success! Perhaps he had been intimidated. But it was probably just his floor.

Nerp: Nerp.

7. The perfect house

Panel 49

Illustration: The five Nerps, out of their disguise, search a quiet street at nightfall.

By the time they left, it was getting late. The five Nerps still had nowhere to stay.

Panel 50

Illustration: A small, abandoned, faded navy-blue house huddles beneath a bridge behind a chipped white fence.

Then they saw it. On a quiet street, right beneath a small bridge: the perfect house.

Panel 51

Illustration: The five Nerps admire the gloomy house and its crooked fence sign.

Faded navy-blue paint. A rusty white fence with chipped paint. A little patch of dead grass. And a large, crooked sign.

Signs and labels: DO NOT TRESPASS

Panel 52

Illustration: Two Nerps carefully straighten the warning sign while the other three supervise.

They straightened the sign. Such a lovely message deserved to hang properly.

Satisfied Nerp: Nerp.

Signs and labels: DO NOT TRESPASS

Panel 53

Illustration: Seen from outside, the cloudy front window barely reveals the sofa immediately behind it.

From outside, you could see through the big front window only when something was very close to it.

Panel 54

Illustration: From inside the house, the street is clearly visible through the same window.

From inside, you could see everything outside perfectly. The Nerps already loved the house’s rustic, sad feel.

Panel 55

Illustration: The tiny five Nerps stand in an impossibly vast interior with a sofa beside the front window.

Then they saw the rest of the inside. It was HUGE. At least five times the size the outside had suggested.

Five impressed Nerps: Nerp…

Panel 56

Illustration: The five Nerps inspect the sofa by the window and a dark doorway in the back-right corner.

One big sofa sat very close to the window. In the back-right corner, a dark doorway led to an enormous back room. The Nerps had an idea.

8. The waiting room

Panel 57

Illustration: Exactly five gloomy Nerps huddle and point toward the dark back room.

They huddled together and discussed it for a minute.

Nerp: Nerp?

Nerp: Nerp.

Nerp: Nerp!

Panel 58

Illustration: All five Nerps assemble an enormous conveyor machine in a flurry of tools.

Then they built their masterpiece. It took about ten seconds.

Sound effect: clank · clonk · done

Panel 59

Illustration: The completed machine has an empty human-sized intake bin, a long conveyor, and an output table.

A bin at the top, big enough for a whole human. A conveyor belt across the room. A table at the end for whatever came out.

Panel 60

Illustration: The empty intake bin looms in shadow. There is no person in or near it.

Their plan was simple and sinister: shoot anyone who trespassed until that person could no longer stand… or breathe. Then put the person into the machine.

Panel 61

Illustration: An open brown cardboard box on the output table contains one colorful toy gun and exactly three blunt orange foam darts.

Somehow, they expected a small brown cardboard box to come out. Inside it: one Nerp gun and three Nerp darts.

Panel 62

Illustration: The striped Nerp holds the harmless toy gun as the other four stand grimly beside the machine.

The gun was still the same harmless foam-dart toy from the elevator. The Nerps’ intentions were another matter entirely.

Panel 63

Illustration: The five distinct Nerps leave the dark workshop and approach the sofa beside the window.

Satisfied with their work, the five returned to the front room. The sofa had space for them all.

Panel 64

Illustration: Exactly five gloomy Nerps sit in a row on one sofa directly behind the front window. No visitor comes. They wait in silence.

And so the five Nerps sat together on the sofa… and waited for their first victim.

The end.
