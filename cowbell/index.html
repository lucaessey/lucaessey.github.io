A normal day

Panel 1

Morning sunlight falls over a quiet farm. Cows graze beside a barn while pigs gather in their pen.

It was a normal day on the farm.

Panel 2

A farmer in navy overalls and a gold plaid shirt goes about his usual chores.

The cows were mooing. The pigs were snorting. Everything was fine.

Sound: MOO. OINK.

Panel 3

The farmer notices an old brass cowbell lying on the barn floor.

Except for the cowbell on the floor.

Panel 4

A close view of the old cowbell’s flat brass face.

There was writing on it.

Inscription: YOU TOUCH ME, I FIND YOU. YOU RING ME, I KILL YOU.

The ring

Panel 5

The farmer lifts the cowbell by its black iron loop.

He picked it up.

Panel 6

Startled, the farmer lets go. The bell falls toward the floor.

Then he dropped it.

Panel 7

The clapper strikes the inside of the falling brass bell.

He heard it ring.

Sound: CLANG

Panel 8

Blackness fills the panel.

And everything went black.

The diagnosis

Panel 9

The farmer opens his eyes in a hospital bed, now wearing a blue gown.

He woke up in a hospital.

Panel 10

The confused farmer looks up at the doctor beside his bed.

Farmer: What happened?

Panel 11

The doctor calmly explains what happened.

Doctor: You had a panic attack.

Panel 12

Back in his overalls, the farmer takes a packet of tea from the doctor before leaving.

The doctor sent him home with some tea.

Reflections

Panel 13

The farmer sits on a brown sofa, holding a shiny metal mug.

At home, he sat on the couch with his tea.

Panel 14

A red face with black horns appears in the mug’s curved reflection, just behind the farmer.

In the reflection of the cup, he saw a devil.

Panel 15

The farmer looks over his shoulder into an empty living room.

He turned around. There was no devil.

Panel 16

In teal pajamas, the farmer freezes with his toothbrush raised. The horned devil is visible behind him only in the mirror.

The next morning, while he was brushing his teeth, he saw it in the mirror.

The passenger

Panel 17

Another morning. The same red devil watches from the bathroom mirror.

The next day, he saw it again.

Panel 18

Exhausted and unshaven, the farmer stares at the same impossible reflection in a darkened bathroom.

And the next. And the next. And the next.

Panel 19

The farmer turns toward a tall red devil standing in the bus aisle among ordinary passengers.

Then, one day on the bus, he saw the devil with his own eyes.

Panel 20

Passengers look at their phones while the horned figure stands plainly beside them. The farmer watches in fear.

No one else seemed to notice. To him, it stuck out like a sore thumb.

Awake

Panel 21

The farmer’s frightened eyes open in darkness, with a faint red reflection.

The next morning, he woke up. The devil was standing right in front of him.

Panel 22

Barefoot in teal pajamas, the farmer stands in a black void facing the towering devil.

But he was not in his bed. He was somewhere completely dark.

Panel 23

In the empty darkness, the devil addresses the farmer.

Devil: Everybody is a book of blood; wherever we’re opened, we’re red.

Panel 24

The devil extends a clawed hand toward the viewer. No injury or aftermath is shown.

Devil: Now it’s your turn to be red.
